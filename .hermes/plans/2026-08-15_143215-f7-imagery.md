# F7 and sub-10 m imagery — implementation plan

> For Hermes: execute this as a protocol, not a literature pass. Do not run the spectral test until the sample table is frozen and the decision rules below are in a committed file.

**Goal:** Decide whether Sentinel-2 NDMI still separates official PCR-positive olives that inspectors marked symptom-absent from PCR-negative olives, and whether any reachable sensor finer than 10 m can do that job better.

**Architecture:** Freeze the 2021 F7 sample from `CAMP_2021.xlsx`. Join it to public rasters. Score pre-registered endpoints. Treat every finer-grain source as a separate, access-gated arm.

**Tech stack:** Python 3 + project `.venv` (rasterio, numpy). Labels from `raw/data/camp_xlsx/CAMP_2021.xlsx`. Sentinel-2 from Earth Search public COGs. Optional Landsat 8 Collection 2 on AWS. Puglia WMS for crown context only.

## Question

F7: among olives that looked healthy to the inspector, does the pixel still look drier when PCR is positive?

If NDMI dies on that subset, the August 2021 result is a damage signal. Do not call it previsual.

## Locked decisions (write these into `nowcast/F7.md` before any join)

1. **Labels.** Source = `CAMP_2021.xlsx` only. Not the CKAN CSV. The xlsx has `SINTOMO` and `ZONA`.
2. **F7+.** `SPECIE` olive, `RISULTATO` starts with Pos, `SINTOMO` starts with Ass, date 2021-06-01 to 2021-09-15, lat/lon finite, inside tile 34TBL valid raster (SCL in {4,5,7}, not fringe).
3. **F7−.** Same except `RISULTATO` starts with Neg. Same date window. Same SCL rule.
4. **Balance.** Keep all F7+ that pass SCL. Draw the same number of F7− with seed 42, stratified by comune when a comune has ≥3 F7+. If a comune has too few negatives, draw from adjacent comuni and flag those rows.
5. **Primary endpoint.** NDMI on `S2A_34TBL_20210812_0_L2A` (already locked). Same rule as before: |Cliff δ| ≥ 0.15 and two-sided permutation p < 0.05. Predicted direction: F7+ lower than F7−.
6. **Must-report secondaries (not optional).**
   - NDVI, NDRE on the same scene.
   - NDMI residualized on NDVI (OLS, pooled).
   - Same points on `S2A_34TBL_20210822_0_L2A`.
   - F7+ vs symptomatic PCR+ (SINTOMO Presente) on NDVI and NDMI. This checks whether “Assente” is optically greener.
   - 3×3 neighborhood: mean NDMI, SD NDMI, fraction SCL in {4,5,7}. Drop a point from the primary table if center pixel is OK but 3×3 veg fraction < 0.4. Report the drop count.
7. **Spatial honesty.** Also report a comune-blocked permutation p (shuffle labels inside comune). If blocked p and iid p disagree, the blocked p wins.
8. **Do not peek.** Do not join 2020 or 2022 F7 points until 2021 F7 is written to `nowcast/cache/f7_2021.csv` and `nowcast/F7.md` has the result table.
9. **Power stop.** If SCL-valid F7+ n < 40, do not run the test. Write the count and stop. Do not pad with symptomatic positives.

Campaign 2021 has 613 olive PCR+ / Assente globally. The tile-and-SCL cut is unknown. Task 1 exists to measure it.

**What “better than 10 m” can actually mean here**

- Finer **map** of which pixel is tree vs soil: Puglia orthophoto WMS + Crecco crowns. Do this. It is free and it attacks mixed pixels.
- Finer **multispectral** on a 2021 date: VHR 2021 and Planet. Blocked without eligibility. Record the block. Do not stall F7 on it.
- Finer **physics** (fluorescence, thermal, 400–900 nm): airborne or commercial. Out of reach this week.
- Super-resolved Sentinel-2: not finer physics. Optional later ablation, not in the F7 primary table.

### Task 4: Mixed-pixel diagnostic from public orthophoto

**Objective:** For each F7 point, estimate how much of the 10 m cell is canopy.

Pull a small RGB chip from the 2019 AGEA WMS around each point (e.g. 30 m). Compute a simple green-excess or ND-like RGB vegetation fraction. Store `rgb_veg_frac` on the CSV. Report NDMI Cliff on the subset with `rgb_veg_frac` ≥ 0.5 vs the full set.

If the WMS fails or rate-limits, write ACCESS_BLOCKED and continue. Do not block F7 on this.

### Task 5: Landsat 8 thermal / NDMI control

**Objective:** Same F7 points, coarser public sensor, August 2021.

Find one Landsat 8 C2 L2 scene over the bbox with cloud < 10% within 7 days of 12 Aug 2021. Sample SR_B4, SR_B5, SR_B6, ST_B10. Compute NDVI, NDMI, LST. Apply the same decision rule. If no scene, write NONE and stop this arm.

### Task 6: Write the result, then stop

Update `nowcast/F7.md` with the table. Three allowed verdicts, nothing else:

- **Previsual-compatible:** NDMI meets both bars on F7+ vs F7−, and blocked p agrees, and 22 Aug replicates.
- **Damage-only:** NDMI fails F7+ vs F7−, or only works when symptomatic trees are included.
- **Inconclusive:** n < 40 after SCL, or 12 Aug and 22 Aug disagree, or blocked vs iid p disagree.

Do not train a classifier. Do not touch 2024–25. Do not email Bari.

## Risks

- Most 2021 Assente positives may sit outside 34TBL or on SCL fringe. Then n < 40 and the test does not run. That is a valid outcome.
- GPS is tree-level but 10 m mixes canopy and soil. Orthophoto fraction is the control. Super-resolution is not a substitute.
- `SINTOMO = Assente` is an inspector call, not qPCR load. Some F7+ may be early disease; some may be lab or field error.
- Crecco labels are visual removal, not PCR. Do not pool them with CAMP.
- VHR / Planet / WV2 look like the obvious upgrade. They are access-blocked for a private computational project with no EU-project wrapper. Ambition here is using every **reachable** photon, not listing sensors we cannot open.
