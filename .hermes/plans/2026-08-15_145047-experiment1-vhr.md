# Current protocol: diagnostic cohort + reachable VHR

**Status:** current plan. Supersedes `.hermes/plans/2026-08-15_143215-f7-imagery.md`.

**Goal:** Test whether an official diagnostic-positive olive has a remotely detectable signature before or around the sample date, after matching for cultivar, place, date, canopy, and drought.

**Not the goal:** Super-resolve Sentinel-2 to 1 m. Treat Crecco disappearance labels as PCR. Email Bari empty-handed.

---

## What is already true

Regione Puglia diagnostic observations are on disk: 12 campaign workbooks, 2013–14 through 2025, tree-level coordinates, cultivar, symptom, official positive/negative result. Call them **diagnostic labels**, not PCR, until each campaign’s assay field is read.

Crecco OQDS-Insight v5 (figshare 28191245): 77,068 crowns, 2,225 photointerpreted infected-or-removed, ~25 km² Brindisi, 2019–2021 WorldView-2. Download is 2.76 GB (v4 size; use latest). Released rasters + vectors exist. Full 8-band commercial WV2 is not established as included.

Puglia ImageServer Export Image works from this machine (HTTP 200, georeferenced PNG). Layers present: 1997, 2006, 2010, 2011, 2013, 2015, **2015_IR**, 2016, 2019, 2022, 2023.

`Ortofoto2015_IR`: 0.15 m, 3-band U8. A live chip over the Brindisi front is color-infrared (vegetation magenta, soil cyan). Band order and radiometry are not calibrated reflectance. Use as vegetation-sensitive 15 cm context in 2015, not as NDMI.

`Ortofoto2022` / `2023`: 0.20 m, 3-band RGB. Confirmed from service metadata.

CLMS `GioLand/VHR_2021_LAEA` ImageServer is public: 2 m, 3-band RGB, Export Image enabled. This is a mosaic service, not the entitlement-gated raw VHR_IMAGE_2021 commercial stack.

Sentinel-2 remains the scalable VNIR / red-edge / SWIR time series. Red-edge and SWIR are 20 m. RGB+NIR are 10 m.

Landsat 8/9 thermal is 100 m native, delivered resampled to 30 m. Do not treat ST_B10 as independent 30 m temperature.

## Spatial unit

The experimental unit is the **tree**, not the 10 m pixel.

1. Take the diagnostic point.
2. Attach a crown from Crecco (inside the 25 km²) or from a Puglia 0.15–0.20 m chip (elsewhere).
3. Measure Sentinel-2 / Sentinel-1 / Landsat on that crown’s pixel neighborhood, weighted by crown/soil fractions from VHR.

Unmixing:

`R_S2(t) ≈ f_olive R_olive(t) + f_soil R_soil(t) + f_other R_other(t)`

`f_*` comes from orthophoto / Crecco. Do not invent 1 m SWIR.

## Experiment 1 (run this)

**Question:** How many days before a positive diagnostic result does out-of-area discrimination stay above matched negative controls?

**Cohort**

- Source: `raw/data/camp_xlsx/CAMP_*.xlsx`.
- Keep olives with finite lat/lon and a dated diagnostic result.
- First cut: points that fall in the Crecco Brindisi footprint (need the figshare bbox after download). If that n is small, expand to all 2021 tile-34TBL olives with crowns from Export Image.
- Label language: `risultato` as written. Symptom and cultivar as covariates.

**Matching (hard)**

Each positive matched to negatives that share:

- sample window (±30 days),
- nearby location (same comune first, else ≤5 km),
- cultivar when both non-empty,
- similar crown area (VHR),
- similar Landsat LST / S2 NDMI neighborhood climate on the same date.

Unmatched positives stay in a residual table. Do not drop them silently.

**Time axis (days from diagnostic date)**

−365, −180, −90, −60, −30, 0, +90.

At each stamp, extract:

- S2: B04, B05, B08, B11, B12, SCL; NDVI, NDRE, NDMI; 3×3 mean and SD; veg fraction.
- S2 residuals vs that tree’s own seasonal baseline (same DOY ±15 d, prior year if available).
- S2 residuals vs matched negatives on the same date.
- S1 VV/VH if a scene exists within 6 days.
- Landsat LST as drought context (100 m native).
- Crown fraction from the nearest Puglia orthophoto year.

**Primary endpoint**

Out-of-area AUROC (or Cliff δ on NDMI residual) at −30, −60, −90 days. Hold out whole comuni, then a later campaign year. No random neighbor split.

**Verdicts**

- Signal dies at 0 days after matching: monitoring only.
- Survives 30–60 days: pre-diagnostic, worth WV3/PRISMA.
- Survives 90+ days: strong.
- Dies after drought/cultivar match: stop remote early-detection. Move effort to the decoder.

**F7 is a slice of this, not a separate religion.** Symptom-absent positives vs symptom-absent negatives on 12 Aug 2021 is the first table inside Experiment 1. Same stop rule: n < 40 after SCL → do not test.
